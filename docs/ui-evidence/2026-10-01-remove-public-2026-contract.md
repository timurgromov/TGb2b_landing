# Remove public 2026 copy contract

Date: 2026-10-01
Routes: `/` and `/novogodniy-korporativ/`
Target: seasonal hero, metadata and root seasonal promo

## Baseline

- Seasonal title and H1 contain `Ведущий на новогодний корпоратив 2026 в Москве`.
- The first seasonal hero tag is `Декабрь 2026`.
- Root promo exposes `Новогодние корпоративы 2026` and `Новогодний сезон 2026`.

## Requested visible delta

- Seasonal title and H1 become `Ведущий на новогодний корпоратив в Москве`.
- The first seasonal hero tag becomes `Декабрь`.
- Root promo becomes `Новогодние корпоративы` / `Новогодний сезон`.
- Public description and structured-data service name no longer contain `2026`.

## Preserved

- Hero subtitle, portrait, tags other than the first, CTA labels and hero layout.
- Three service packages, proof cases, letters, FAQ and lead form.
- The form remains restricted to December 2026 because it is operational season logic, not public offer copy.
- The internal CRM lead marker remains tied to the 2026 campaign.
- Pricing remains unimplemented.

## Acceptance

- No public seasonal marketing copy contains `2026` on either route.
- Footer copyright and operational date constraints may still contain `2026`.
- Desktop and mobile heroes have no horizontal overflow or broken CTA layout.
- Seasonal title, description and JSON-LD service name have no year.
